# RP Launcher privacy policy

*Last updated: October 2026. RP Launcher is made by Netgician LLC (netgician.com).*

**RP Launcher collects nothing.** It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking,
and it doesn't have Android's internet permission, so it can't send anything anywhere by
itself. Everything it does happens on your phone.

## What it uses on your phone, and why

Android asks you before RP Launcher can use any of these. You can say no to any of them;
only the feature that needs it stops working.

| What | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Your apps list | To show your apps as tiles | Stays on the phone |
| Camera | The See tile (magnifier and reading text aloud), and photos you choose to text | Stays on the phone unless you text a photo |
| Microphone | Voice messages you record | Only to the people you send them to |
| Contacts | To show names instead of numbers, and to pick people to call or text | Stays on the phone |
| Texts (SMS and MMS) | Only if you make RP Launcher your texting app: to send, receive and show your texts | Kept in the phone's own message store, like any texting app; sent only to the people you text, through your carrier |
| Calls and call history | Only if you make RP Launcher your phone app: to show the call screen and recent calls | Stays on the phone |
| Location | Only for SOS and Where am I, and only while you use them | Only in texts that you or SOS send to the people you chose |
| Notifications | Only if you turn on Alerts: to read your notifications aloud | Kept in memory only, never saved or sent |

## Services of your phone that RP Launcher asks for help

Some features use services built into your phone. Those services are run by your phone's
maker (on most phones, Google) under their own privacy policies:

- **Speech** (reading aloud) uses your phone's text-to-speech voice.
- **Speak** (typing a text by voice) uses your phone's speech recognition.
- **Street addresses** (Where am I and SOS) use your phone's address lookup, which sends the
  location to your phone's location service to find the nearest address.
- **Texts and calls** go through your phone and your mobile carrier, as with any phone.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling RP Launcher removes its settings. Your texts and call history stay in the
phone's shared stores, as they would with any texting or phone app, so the next app you
use still has them.

## Children

RP Launcher is not aimed at children and collects no information about anyone.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
